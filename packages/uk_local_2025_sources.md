Every artifact manifest records its source URL, extraction timestamp, SHA-256, byte size and uploaded R2 location. Full-row parsers retain physical source-row coordinates in the lineage key and cell note; selectors address the compact virtual-cell table with its header on row 1.

The default merged bundle grows from 426,058 to 1,000,748 facts, adding 574,690 facts (+134.9%; 2.35 times the previous volume), and from 278 to 318 source packages. The baseline includes the latest New Zealand and consumption publisher additions on main. Consumers should assess ingestion time and memory before a Microcosm pin bump. Moving generated record-set declarations to R2 is a possible follow-up architecture change; this change retains the existing Git-based package declarations.

Current declarations explicitly replace superseded publisher observations. The retired May 2025 UC snapshots contain 5,406 overlapping observations (4,424 constituency-child, 632 constituency and 350 local-authority) plus 632 omitted legacy-query marker rows. The revised NRS edition replaces 5,244 older 2024 Persons cells, and the latest DfI edition replaces 12 receipts and six Ulsterbus journeys. ONS MYE3 replaces the thirteen original mid-2024 Persons/all-age totals exported by the older package; its three additional scopes remain under distinct revised spec IDs. The 3,575 older ONS detailed age/sex cells remain because MYE3 does not republish those cuts. Both new ONS packages use the workbook’s April 2024 boundary vintage, independently of their October 2026 release. Remaining semantic duplicate groups number 2,324; distinct census cuts and older-only series retain truthful provenance. Historical Git pins and immutable raw artifacts in R2 preserve reproduction of superseded editions without exporting them as current facts.

The three new NRS/NISRA population packages use the established `ons.mid_year_population_estimate` concept, `resident_population` domain and role, and lower-case `sex` identifiers. Total-sex and all-age axes are omitted, and the open band uses `age=90_plus` with the publisher label “90 and over”. This intentionally aligns their concept and dimension identities with existing population selectors; publisher names, artifacts, values, reference periods, geography vintages and provenance classes remain intact. NISRA's raw numeric age code 90 additionally carries `age >= 90` in years. Its interpretation requires the pinned workbook, the unchanged row/code guard, an explicit band-axis declaration and the literal Contents!A20 definition, preserved as one additional evidence cell. The original Flat rows, cells and fact lineage remain unchanged. Older retained cuts' `census` versus newer packages' `model_output` classifications remain a separate follow-up. Source-spec migration changes source-series identities: downstream compiled references should be refreshed when updating the Chronicle pin. Compatibility scopes remain exact—NRS 2024 Persons, UC May 2025, ONS thirteen equivalent 2024 totals and DfI historical Ulsterbus journey cells. No target aggregation or cross-publisher reconciliation is performed.

## Raw geography and rent evidence

The following directories under `db/data/` have `registration_kind: raw_only` and no fact-package alias:

- `voa/brma_boundaries_2020`: original English GML ZIP, applicable May 2020.
- `welshgov/brma_boundaries_2012`: May 2012 Rent Officers Wales boundaries, Edinburgh DataShare deposit, including correspondence and OGL documentation.
- `scotgov/brma_boundaries_2009`: Scottish 2009 polygons revised July 2015.
- `scotgov/brma_postcode_lookup_2023`: Rent Service Scotland FOI postcode-to-BRMA lookup.
- `ons/lsoa2021_population_weighted_centroids_v4`: all 35,672 EW LSOA centroids from the ONS V4 feature-service download; publisher ONS/Ordnance Survey licence applies.
- `nrs/oa2022_population_weighted_centroids`: original NRS Census 2022 OA centroid ZIP.
- `voa/shadow_list_of_rents_april_2026`: original 502,239 weekly net rent records, October 2024–September 2025.
- `welshgov/shadow_list_of_rents_2023`: original ATISN 25142 workbook, October 2022–September 2023.
- `scotgov/brma_market_evidence_2016_2021`: original FOI 2016–2021 letting workbook, including postcode, BRMA, payment frequency and net rent.

The rent lists retain payment frequency and source units. PIPR, rent-officer lists and Scottish rent statistics share an evidence base; their separate statistics do not establish independence. Chronicle constructs no OA-to-BRMA assignment, overlap, rent distribution or BRMA private-renter estimate.

## Validation

Run the source package at its `artifact_year`, rather than restamping a census or revised estimate to 2025:

```sh
uv run chronicle validate-package ons-lad-population-by-age-2025 --year 2025
uv run chronicle build-suite nrs-census2022-uv101a-ukpc24 --year 2022 --out /tmp/uv101a --replace
uv run pytest -q tests/test_chronicle_uk_local_2025.py
```

`build-suite` validates source rows/cells, selector guards, raw facts, consumer contracts and database lineage. Registry aliases include each fact package in the UK bundle; raw-only registrations remain artifact inputs.
