The source is MSD's *Annual Report 2025*, covering the year ended 30 June
2025. Chronicle stores the July–June fiscal period as `fiscal_year:2024`, the
opening year, with the publisher's table header `2025` in
`source_period_label`. These are actual expenses, in NZD thousands; the
package normalizes the unit to NZD with `value_scale: 1000`.

PDF page 182, extracted lines 13–18, identifies the table and column units:

> Benefits or Related Expenses
> Benefits or related expenses includes the following significant expenditure types:
> Actual Actual
> 2024 2025
> $000 $000
> Benefits or Related Expenses

The package selects the second numeric column of the eleven expense rows on
page 182, including the publisher's Other Benefits or Related Expenses and
Total Benefits or Related Expenses. It does not compute or add expense rows.
Page 182, extracted line 19, states:

> 2,410,658 Accommodation Assistance 2,232,026

Page 182, extracted line 29, states:

> 35,663,484 Total Benefits or Related Expenses 38,267,536

The annual report does **not** define which payments the Accommodation
Assistance appropriation includes. A search of all 210 pages finds this
name only in the page 182 expenses table, the page 189 appropriation
expenditure table, and the page 206 list of appropriations not reported.
Page 206, extracted lines 20–21, explains the exemption:

> Exempted from reporting under section 15D(2)(b)(ii) of the Public
> Finance Act 1989 (Note 2)

Page 206, extracted lines 24–25, names:

> Non-departmental benefit or related expenses:
> Accommodation Assistance

Therefore these facts retain **Accommodation Assistance** as the published
component label. They do not establish that this expense is Accommodation
Supplement alone, or assert an unverified list of included payments. The
requested payment-definition evidence remains unavailable from the staged
artifact. The candidate missing artifact is the Vote Social Development
2024/25 Estimates of Appropriations:
`https://www.treasury.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2024-05/estimates24-v10-socdev.pdf`.
That candidate URL has not been fetched or verified; the report's embedded
links contain Budget initiative summaries, not a link to those Estimates.
