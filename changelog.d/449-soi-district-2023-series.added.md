`soi-congressional-district-2023` gets the record sets chronicle#448 gave the
TY2022 district package: 45 more columns of `23incd.csv` (the 15 state-twin
columns and 30 columns without a state twin, with the TY2022 measure ids,
concepts and labels) and returns and AGI in the nine AGI classes. The TY2023
guide says the districts are those of the 118th Congress, so the package is its
own series: `5001800US` GEOIDs, `118th_congress` vintage, and its own
`record_set_spec_id`s for the all-returns and class record sets. Its file's
nine classes add up to the all-class row, and stub 9 is the guide's stubs 9 and
10 together, as in TY2022. The package leaves out the file's EITC total
(`A59660`, 481 facts): in every row it adds the three-or-more-children amount
in dollars to the other three amounts in thousands. It reads the four amounts
by number of children as published. See the entry on the district totals for
the rows that give facts (56,049 facts in all).
