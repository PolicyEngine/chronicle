Add the next editions of two BEA packages (chronicle#449).
`bea-nipa-total-wages-salaries-2026-11-25` reads series A034RC (wages and
salaries) for 2018, 2023 and 2024 from `NipaDataA.txt` as BEA served it after
its 25 November 2026 release. `bea-regional-state-personal-income-components-2026-09-30`
reads the eight SAINC5N lines for the United States, the 50 states and DC from
the `SAINC.zip` of BEA's 30 September 2026 release, for the year it is built
for. Each declares the concepts, units, rows, filters and constraints of
`bea-nipa-total-wages-salaries` or `bea-regional-state-personal-income-components-2024`,
and its record ids carry the release date. BEA's 2026 annual update revised
wages and salaries for 2021 to 2025 and the state statistics for 2009 to 2025.
