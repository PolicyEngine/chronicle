Add the next editions of five BEA packages (chronicle#449). Four read
`NipaDataA.txt` as BEA served it after its 25 November 2026 release:
`bea-nipa-total-wages-salaries-2026-11-25` (series A034RC for 2018, 2023 and
2024), `bea-nipa-personal-income-components-2026-11-25` (18 series),
`bea-nipa-personal-income-disposition-2026-11-25` (6 series) and
`bea-nipa-pension-contributions-2026-11-25` (2 series), the last three for the
year they are built for. `bea-regional-state-personal-income-components-2026-09-30`
reads the eight SAINC5N lines for the United States, the 50 states and DC from
the `SAINC.zip` of BEA's 30 September 2026 release, for the year it is built
for. Each declares the concepts, units, rows, filters and constraints of the
package it follows, and its record ids carry the release date. Against the
earlier files, the national series change only for 2021 to 2025 (the defined
contribution series also gain 2025), and the state statistics for 2009 to 2025
were revised.
