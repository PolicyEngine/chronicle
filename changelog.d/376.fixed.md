One dimension value now carries one label, whichever packages and build years a consumer combines. Every value that two or more packages emit, or that its packages word more than one way, has one label in Chronicle's register, `chronicle/dimension_values/canonical_labels.json` (4,824 values). Facts take that label in `dimension_value_labels` and `layout.groupby_value_label` when they are built, and keep the package's own wording, where it differs, in the new optional `publisher_dimension_value_labels`; rows are emitted as `chronicle.consumer_fact.v5`, v4 plus that field. The IRS filing-season week-47 rows for adjusted gross income `under_1` keep the IRS header "No adjusted gross income and deficit" as their label and in that field, and their facts carry "No adjusted gross income", as every other IRS SOI package does. A value labelled two ways is an error, never a warning: `validate-package` and `build-suite` refuse one that a package labels two ways, and `build-bundle` also refuses one that two packages label differently; the list of accepted label variants is gone. No fact key or value moves (chronicle#376).
