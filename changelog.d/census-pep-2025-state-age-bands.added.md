Add Census's Vintage 2025 state population by age group (SC-EST2025-ALLDATA6) as `census-pep-2025-state-age-bands`: the July 1, 2024 resident population of each state and DC, in all and in fifteen age groups that partition it (single years 0 to 5, then 6 to 11, 12 to 17, 18 to 24, ten-year groups from 25 to 34 through 55 to 64, 65 to 74, and 75 and over), 816 facts. Each group sums the both-sexes, all-origins rows of the six race groups at its ages, and `packages/census/pep_2025_state_age_bands/build_package.py` writes the package from the Census file. The label register gives `age_0` to `age_5`, which the Census population projections package also emits, one label each. PolicyEngine/microcosm#1528 calibrates the ACS local-area release to these counts.
